# Assignment transport

[GitHub issues](https://github.com/WigglyDon/rust-reps/issues) own live assignments,
amendments, progress, questions and handback. Direct Don instructions remain
valid. Read relevant amendments on entry and before conflicting changes or
integration. A shared account and a later timestamp do not establish authority.

Routine work needs no manual packet relay or archive. A standalone packet may
transport an explicit instruction when needed, but does not become a second
mutable task ledger. Keep it complete and literal, separate claims from observed
facts, and preserve unknown state. Historical `packet-verify` checks the old
schema only; its fields do not impose the retired supervisor/worker workflow.

[Development](../DEVELOPMENT.md) owns execution and publication boundaries.
